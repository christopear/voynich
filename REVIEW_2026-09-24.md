**Voynich manuscript: independent review and reproduction audit — 24 September 2026**

**Follow-up: supplied boundary.py.** After the initial review, the user added code/boundary.py. It implements the previously missing visible-to-hidden boundary experiment and exactly reproduces 769 candidate joined types, 624 jointly evaluable cases, 399/624 correct stem-only predictions (63.94%) and 442/624 correct stem-plus-initial predictions (70.83%). The statements below about this driver being absent describe the initial package and are superseded for this experiment. The other missing experiment drivers remain unverified.

The additional context corrects 89 baseline errors and introduces 46 new errors, a net gain of 43 predictions, or 6.89 percentage points. This is now a reproducible exploratory finding. Output and candidate details are saved as boundary_report.txt and boundary_candidates.json under results/review_2026-09-24/.

The implementation genuinely fits terminal predictions using only parsed within-line token pairs, without inserting inferred internal cuts into those training counts. It correctly compares both predictors on the same eligible cases. However, boundary selection is not fully independent of terminal information: it requires n/l/r endings, attestation of the exact terminal-bearing left component, and maximizes the product of both component frequencies. Candidate selection and model fitting use the same full corpus. Training the model before constructing candidates does not itself provide independence or a held-out test. Also, parsed separators include uncertain commas and illustration interruptions, so calling every training boundary an ordinary visible space overstates what the parser preserves.

I ran a limited sensitivity check, excluding all pages containing each evaluated joined type from predictor fitting while retaining the original candidate selections. On 620 jointly evaluable cases, stem-only accuracy was 393/620 (63.39%), versus 435/620 (70.16%) with the next initial: +6.77 percentage points. This is encouraging evidence that same-page training alone does not explain the gain. It is not end-to-end held-out validation because segmentation still uses full-corpus frequencies. Diagnostic counts are saved in boundary_diagnostics.json.

The 624 original cases share 86 stems and 237 stem/initial combinations; independent-observation significance calculations would neglect that grouping. A stronger validation should freeze the rules, separate training and evaluation by folio/quire, use terminal-marginalized or otherwise independently established cut selection, rerun selection under suitable structured nulls, and preserve transcription uncertainty. The inherited parser failure discussed below is present in this file too. No source code was altered during this follow-up.

Revised judgment: the missing calculation has been recovered and its numerical claim verified. It supports a shared terminal/initial association at visible and proposed internal boundaries under this selection procedure. It does not establish genuine latent boundaries, phonological sandhi, or meaningful plaintext.

The folder contains a useful exploratory research programme, with some reproducible statistical findings and a potentially interesting boundary-transfer hypothesis. It does not establish a decipherment, a source language, semantic equivalence classes, or the superiority of a meaningful-cipher explanation over structured pseudotext. The broad architecture proposed in the handoff substantially overlaps prior research.

**Interpretive and implementation issues**

1. Boundary localization is not boundary discovery. Script 02 constructs positive examples by joining two known spaced tokens, then retains cases with sufficiently frequent pieces and an eligible true cut. Success on that task does not prove naturally unspaced strings consist of two units. The reported real-token negative class is also uncertain: if the hypothesis is correct, some natural tokens in that class are themselves compounds.

2. The threshold -2.1654 is hard-coded. Its calibration is described but not supplied. Excluding f3r from frequency estimates does not erase any influence of repeated inspection of f3r on model design. The current leave-one-page-out evaluation can also retain the opposite side of the same folio; it is not leave-one-folio- or leave-one-quire-out evaluation.

3. The permutation test shuffles within stems, not within stem × page/hand/section/line-position strata. Its strong result establishes association under that null, but not a causal production rule or phonological sandhi. The latter term presupposes a sound system that has not been identified. Moreover, significance within lines and nonsignificance across lines do not alone constitute a direct test that the effects differ. The cross-line sample is much smaller.

4. The parser discards distinctions important to this research. Periods and uncertain comma separators become equivalent; question marks are removed; uncommon glyph codes are replaced by spaces. A concrete compound-alternative failure occurs in the downloaded corpus: `dai[{cto}:@194;]y` becomes `['daicto', 'y']` instead of the first-reading string `['daictoy']`. This demonstrates an artificial token boundary. Its total effect was not measured, so it should not be assumed either negligible or fatal. The original code is preserved for reproducibility. Zandbergen's [IVTFF documentation](https://www.voynich.nu/extra/sp_transcr.html) explains why these editorial distinctions matter.

5. Script 03 removes terminal distinctions even though the handoff correctly says they may carry information. This is legitimate for exploratory family discovery, but cannot establish the final latent representation. Its context vectors flatten lines and pages into one sequence and pool all uncommon neighbours into `L:*` and `R:*`; shared catch-all dimensions can inflate cosine similarity. Comparisons need frequency-matched baselines and ablations.

6. More shuffle-corrected mutual information after normalization is not recovered meaning. The transformation changes categories, estimation bias and sometimes segmentation. The data-processing inequality applies to true mutual information under fixed deterministic mappings; the reported difference-from-null statistic is a different quantity. Even a real improvement in held-out prediction would establish predictability, not semantics.

7. Context distinguishes some OK/OT forms, but that does not disprove every homophony model. Two encodings of the same plaintext unit can be chosen according to context, scribe or table. The test bears most directly on context-independent exchangeability. Conversely, failure to detect a contextual difference does not prove equality: a proper equivalence test requires a specified tolerance and adequate power.

8. Naibbe is a positive control designed to resemble Voynich. It tests whether a method can work in one known setting; it is not an unbiased test of which mechanism generated Voynich. Random interior shuffles and random vocabulary collapses are useful but weak negative controls because they destroy much of the positional grammar already known to exist. Structured generators and independent cipher families are needed.

**Comparison with research outside the folder**

Currier's 1976 paper already explicitly described dependence between word-final symbols and the following word's initial, as well as the line as a functional entity. Thus, the handoff's terminal dependency belongs to a much older research lineage than its 2026 repository comparison implies. The stem-preserving permutation implementation is a useful refinement and replication. [Currier's original paper](https://voynich.nu/extra/curr_main.html).

Stolfi's 2000 grammar already classified apparent combinations of normal words among exceptional tokens, and proposed layered internal structure. The existence of joined forms and compositional families is therefore not newly discovered here. [Stolfi's grammar](https://www.voynich.nu/hist/stolfi/grammar.html).

The broader contrast between language-like token statistics, unusually predictable glyph sequences and weak token order was described by [Reddy and Knight (2011)](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511/) and developed in [Lindemann and Bowern's entropy study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14697). The handoff's Latin optimizer failures are useful negative results but not a new exclusion of simple substitution.

[Montemurro and Zanette (2013)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0066344) found structured word distributions and co-occurrence networks. [Layfield and Davis (2026)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405027080_The_Application_of_Latent_Semantic_Analysis_to_the_Voynich_Manuscript), published in Digital Humanities Quarterly, report coherence patterns using latent semantic analysis, while explicitly distinguishing measured similarity from recovered meaning. These support organized content as a possibility, not a translation.

Meaninglessness is not equivalent to randomness. [Timm and Schinner's generator](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01611194.2019.1596999) and [Gaskell and Bowern's human-generated gibberish experiment](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3313/paper4.pdf) demonstrate why some language-like statistics can arise without a linguistic message. The latter is a particularly important counterweight absent from the handoff's central argument.

[Greshko's Naibbe work (2025)](https://github.com/greshko/naibbe-cipher) provides a reversible, manually executable verbose homophonic cipher with many Voynich-like statistics. The general existence proof belongs to Greshko. Applying the folder's boundary method to its known deleted spaces is a useful additional benchmark.

[Kinnison's positional-entropy article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01611194.2026.2697318) was published online on 7 September 2026. Its accessible publisher metadata and abstract describe a verbose-cipher comparison. I could verify that bibliographic claim, but did not obtain its full methods and therefore do not treat its numerical conclusions as audited here.

An especially relevant addition is [Rozanova and Temerev's August 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17096). It investigates units, separator uncertainty and edge dependence with cipher and pseudotext controls and image-based checks. This directly overlaps the handoff's broad representational thesis. It remains a preprint, not settled consensus; it makes claims of novelty for that broad thesis particularly difficult.

The [Workwrite-Niidome repository](https://github.com/Workwrite-Niidome/voynich-manuscript-analysis) is an exploratory AI-assisted project, not established peer-reviewed confirmation. Its reported sandhi architecture cannot by itself confer scientific validation on the folder's interpretation.

**Best current assessment**

As an object, the manuscript is a medieval illustrated codex. Radiocarbon evidence dates the parchment to the early fifteenth century, conventionally 1404–1438; that dates animal material, not ink directly. [University of Arizona's account](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/624074). Botanical, astronomical/zodiacal and pharmaceutical-looking imagery motivates a working interpretation as a compendium of natural or medical knowledge, but exact subject matter is not translated. [Yale's collection description](https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/beinecke/collections/beinecke-cipher-voynich-manuscript).

The influential five-scribe attribution is a paleographic interpretation rather than an observed count of named authors. [Davis's 2020 study](https://doi.org/10.1353/mns.2020.0011). The 2024 multispectral work clarified faded marginal alphabets and ownership evidence; it did not reveal a working decryption key. [Davis's own account](https://manuscriptroadtrip.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/multispectral-imaging-and-the-voynich-manuscript/).

As text, Voynichese is demonstrably constrained and affected by position and layout. The glyph/token/space correspondences must be tested rather than assumed. A meaningful language under an unusual writing or encipherment system is plausible; technical notation, abbreviation, and structured pseudotext remain alternatives. No specific language, plaintext unit inventory or semantic interpretation is established by this folder or by the current sources reviewed.

The potentially strongest narrow contribution is the claimed transfer of a terminal-prediction rule from visible spaces to inferred internal boundaries. To establish it, recover its driver, freeze inference and scoring rules, remove dependence on the tested terminal cue when selecting cuts, use folio/quire-held-out data, compare structured negative controls and multiple transcriptions, and report uncertainty and coverage. It could become a valuable result about boundary structure even if no translation follows.

The defensible advance is therefore an operational research method and several replicated constraints. A novel, externally validated cryptanalytic breakthrough has not yet been demonstrated.
